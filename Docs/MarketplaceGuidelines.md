# Cursor Studio Marketplace Guidelines

Marketplace exists for safe, original cursor themes that work well on macOS.
Listings may be removed or restricted when they violate these rules.

## Allowed content

- Original cursor artwork created by the publisher.
- Artwork the publisher is explicitly licensed to redistribute.
- Clear adaptations of public-domain or permissively licensed material when
  the source and license are credited.
- Themes that use Cursor Studio's declarative package format and stay within
  documented image, animation, and compatibility limits.

## Prohibited content

- Malware, scripts, executables, plug-ins, installers, or instructions intended
  to bypass macOS security.
- Packages that read, alter, or reference files outside their own theme.
- Stolen artwork, trademark impersonation, or misleading affiliation.
- Harassment, hate content, sexual exploitation, graphic illegal content, or
  content that endangers people.
- Invisible, deceptive, or inaccessible cursors presented without a clear
  warning.
- Spam, keyword stuffing, manipulated download counts, or duplicate listings
  intended to occupy publication slots.
- Personal information included without permission.

## Listing quality

Listings must use accurate screenshots, a descriptive title, a useful
description, the correct category and tags, and honest compatibility metadata.
Creators must disclose static animation fallbacks or missing system roles.

Verification means that identity or package checks were completed; it is not an
endorsement of taste, uninterrupted availability, or compatibility with every
future macOS version.

## Enforcement

Cursor Studio may reject a version, unlist or suspend a theme, restrict a
creator, preserve evidence required to handle a report, and cooperate with
valid legal requests. Creators may appeal by using the support channel once it
is published.
